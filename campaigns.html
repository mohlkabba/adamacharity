Project 3 · Ongoing · London

Mosque Fall Prevention & Accessibility Project

YADAMA is working with a London mosque to improve accessibility for older worshippers with reduced mobility and longstanding chronic pain. Following an Occupational Therapy assessment, support equipment has been supplied and fitted while the project continues to raise awareness of falls prevention and promote safe mobility within mosque facilities.

Partner with this project

Impact goals

  • Increase independence for older worshippers
  • Prevent falls and avoidable injuries
  • Promote a safe, accessible worship environment

Who the project supports

  • Older worshippers
  • People with arthritis affecting the hands, fingers, wrists or knees, and those experiencing hip or shoulder pain
  • People with mobility difficulties
  • People recovering from a stroke
  • Anyone at increased risk of falling

Risks identified

An informal observation identified several environmental hazards:

  • Wet flooring in toilet and ablution areas
  • No non-slip mats or grab rails
  • No emergency call system in toilet facilities
  • Unstable folding chairs without armrests, increasing risk during sit-to-stand transfers

Proposed interventions

  • Complete an Occupational Therapy environmental assessment
  • Provide non-slip mats in key areas
  • Recommend grab rails and drop-down support rails
  • Recommend high-back chairs with armrests for safer transfers
  • Install an emergency call-bell system
  • Provide fall-prevention education and practical advice

Expected outcomes

  • Reduced risk of falls and avoidable hospital admissions
  • Greater independence and confidence for older users
  • Safer toileting and sit-to-stand transfers
  • Improved accessibility throughout mosque facilities
  • A safer worship environment that protects dignity and wellbeing

Project timeline

Delivered within one to two months

The assessment, recommendations and initial installation were completed within one to two months. The support equipment and emergency call-bell system are now in place, while accessibility monitoring and falls-prevention awareness continue.

2026 programme delivered Completed

The 2026 programme delivered food support in Port Loko and hot meals in Freetown.

Between February and March 2026, YADAMA distributed 100 Ramadan food packs to vulnerable older adults in Port Loko District.

The programme also served 200 hot meals — Jollof rice, chicken and bottled water — to people experiencing homelessness, people who are blind, and people living with disabilities in Freetown.

Who we're serving

Widows

Women raising families alone, with limited income and no financial support.

Elderly

Older residents living alone, unable to work, and without regular access to food.

Disabled Families

Households where disability limits earning potential and access to resources.

Neuro / Stroke Rehabilitation & Mother & Baby Care Project

Neuro Rehabilitation Project

Supporting selected hospital stroke rehabilitation wards by delivering 50 Neuro-Rehabilitation Resource Packages.

Each package will include:

  1. Resistance therapy band
  2. Soft hand exercise ball
  3. Therapy putty
  4. Illustrated home exercise programme
  5. Stroke and caregiver education booklet
Our Goal

To improve recovery outcomes for stroke survivors while supporting hospitals with limited rehabilitation resources and helping individuals regain independence in their daily lives.

Mother & Baby Care Project

Supporting mothers and newborn babies in maternity wards who are unable to afford essential items following childbirth.

Many families are discharged from hospital without the essential items needed to care for a newborn. Through this project, we aim to provide practical support during one of the most important moments in a family's life.
