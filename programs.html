Food Distribution

Humanitarian Relief

When families face hardship, we respond with food, clothing, hygiene supplies, emergency assistance and community-based humanitarian support.

During February and March 2026, YADAMA distributed 100 Ramadan food packs to vulnerable older adults in Port Loko District and served 200 hot meals to people experiencing homelessness, people who are blind, and people living with disabilities in Freetown.

  • Ramadan annual food distribution campaign
  • Emergency response food parcels
  • Direct delivery to households — no middlemen
  • Support for older adults, widows, orphaned children and disabled people
  • Volunteer-packed and volunteer-delivered

Rehabilitation Support

Therapy, Equipment & Long-term Care

We improve access to rehabilitation services, mobility aids, assistive devices, adaptive equipment, occupational therapy, physiotherapy and community-based rehabilitation programmes.

Rehabilitation restores more than physical function. It supports confidence, participation, independence and dignity for children and adults living with disabilities or recovering from illness and injury.

  • Wheelchairs, walking frames and mobility aids
  • Hand therapy and support for hand and wrist function
  • Support for neurological, orthopaedic and rheumatological conditions
  • Rehabilitation following traumatic hand injuries
  • Long-term follow-up and support visits
  • Partnership with local health workers
  • Priority given to those with no other support network

Donate equipment

We gratefully accept donations of mobility and rehabilitation equipment to support patients requiring long-term care in Sierra Leone, including wheelchairs, walking sticks, walking frames, crutches, commode chairs, and other assistive devices.

Your support helps improve independence, dignity, and quality of life for those living with disabilities.

Disability Inclusion

Access, Participation & Equal Opportunity

Disability should never prevent someone from learning, working, joining community life or accessing compassionate care. We help remove practical and social barriers by combining appropriate equipment with advocacy, education and inclusive programme design.

Our work supports people across ages and backgrounds, with care shaped around individual priorities and the meaningful activities of everyday life.

  • Inclusive access to humanitarian assistance
  • Mobility and adaptive equipment matched to individual needs
  • Support for children and adults living with disabilities
  • Awareness that promotes dignity and social inclusion
Partner for Inclusion
Community Partnerships

Stronger Communities, Sustainable Change

We work alongside local communities, healthcare providers and partner organisations to promote inclusion, improve health and wellbeing, strengthen local capacity and create sustainable opportunities for future generations.

Community knowledge guides our priorities. By listening first and collaborating throughout, we build support that is relevant, respectful and designed to last.

  • Seasonal clothing drives for families in need
  • Welfare check visits to elderly and isolated individuals
  • Community events and gatherings
  • Referrals to local health and social services
  • Volunteer-led outreach and companionship

How we deliver aid

3

4
